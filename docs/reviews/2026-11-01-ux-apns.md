# UX/design-polish review: P4 APNs wake-up notifications (r5-relay 78e34e5)

Reviewer: Claude Opus (`claude-opus-5-5`). I did not write this code and only reviewed it; no files were changed, no simulator was booted and nothing was built.

**Inputs:**
- Pre-push UI suite screenshots 01–08 plus 03b and 05a (iPhone Air).
- `PeopleView.swift`, with the row at :156 and :257.
- `CompanionCoordinator.swift`: `pushForegroundTick`, `notificationMomentReached`, `peopleTabAppeared`, and the call sites at :992, :1181 and :1458.
- `PushWakeup.swift`, `PushNotificationSupport.swift` and the `ContentView.swift` wake handler.
- `relay/src/service.ts` `pushPlan` and `relay/src/reducer.ts` (how approvals and declines change a request's status).
- The gate docs.

**Not seen:** the denied row, the system prompt, real banners and Settings. None of these are in the shot set, so they were reviewed from code.

## P1 (must fix)
- **P1-1. The "declined" banner can be false.**
  - **The bug:** `pushPlan` (service.ts:176) always sends `Declined your request.` But the reducer (:414–418) only marks a request declined when *every* approver declines, and one approval makes it approved. So with several approvers:
    - an owner who is still waiting on others is told it was declined;
    - an owner who has *already been approved* can then get "Declined your request." from a second person, and may think they can't end the lock.
  - **Fix in `pushPlan`:** read `ledger.requests[approval.requestID].request` after the commit and branch on it:
    - status `approved`: send nothing for a decline;
    - status `pending` (others haven't answered yet): body `Declined. Others you asked can still answer.`;
    - status `declined`: `Declined your request.`
  - **Related (P3):** a second approval after the request is already approved re-sends "Approved…". Send nothing when the status was already `approved` before this approval.

## P2 (should fix)
- **P2-1. The "approved" banner promises an action that may not exist.** `Approved your request. Open Phone Lockbox to end your lock.` is wrong in two cases:
  - The developer gate "Friend approval can end locks (prototype)" is off, which is the default. People then says "It never ends your lock by itself in this build" and shows no End lock now.
  - The lock has already ended.

  Fix: body `Approved ending your lock early.` Let the app's Waiting card decide what action to offer.
- **P2-2. The prompt after Create invite link covers the link the owner wants to share.** `notificationMomentReached()` runs as soon as `pendingInvite` is set. The system dialog lands on top of Share invite link / Copy link (screenshot 01's state), and iOS shows no purpose string. The owner can't tell why it's asking now.
  - Fix: defer this moment until the owner comes back to the app after sharing. Set a flag on create and fire on the next scene `.active` while `pendingInvite` is live, or after Copy link is tapped.
  - Add one footnote line to the live-link state, under "The link works once, for one hour.": `Phone Lockbox will ask to notify you when they join.`
  - Accept and Ask now are fine where they are: the person has just acted and the reason is obvious.
- **P2-3. The fourth moment (People appears, already helping someone, undecided) is a cold system prompt.** It fires on tab appear with no on-screen reason, and it can also land on top of a freshly opened invite card. Replace the automatic prompt with an inline card in the notices slot (below the Waiting card, above People you're helping):
  - Title: `Get notified when Dana asks`
  - Body: `Otherwise you'll only see requests when you open People.`
  - Buttons: primary `Turn on notifications` (this calls `requestIfUndecided`), plain `Not now` (hides the card for that launch, or for 7 days).
  - Keep `PushPromptPolicy.shouldPromptOnPeopleAppear` as the visibility rule for the card.
- **P2-4. The denied-row copy is wrong for owners.** `Notifications are off, so you won't hear about requests.` An owner never gets requests; they get answers and joins. Make the copy depend on role, using real names when there is exactly one person:
  - Helping only: `Notifications are off, so you won't hear when Dana asks.`
  - Trusted people only: `Notifications are off, so you won't hear when Ari answers.`
  - Both, or several people: `Notifications are off, so you won't hear about requests or answers.`

## P3 (polish)
- **P3-1. The denied row is a full card with a full-width secondary button.** That is heavier than the plan's "one quiet row".
  - Use `ViewThatFits { HStack { Label…; Spacer; Button("Turn On").buttonStyle(.bordered).controlSize(.small) }; VStack {…} }`.
  - "Turn On" says what the tap does. "Open Settings" is the mechanism.
  - Keep the accessibility label `Open Settings to turn notifications on`. Footnote text with `fixedSize(vertical)` is fine for Dynamic Type.
  - Placement (after the people list, before reinvite/invite) is right. It would be the second-to-last card when an owner has one person, but the 24 pt spacer plus 03b's clearance suggest the tab bar won't cover it. Add a denied-state screenshot to the suite (a launch argument that forces `.denied`) so this is seen next time.
- **P3-2. Request banner wording.** `Asks you to approve ending a lock early.` should be `Asks you to approve ending their lock early.` In-app, the Waiting card says "Dana is asking for help" (06). Align it to `Dana asks to end their lock early` so the banner and the card match.
- **P3-3. Fallback title.** When a name is missing, the title `Phone Lockbox` repeats the banner's app header. Use `Your trusted person` for answer/joined and `Someone you help` for request.
- **P3-4. Foreground banner on People.** When the scene is active and People is the selected tab, skip the banner and sound and just refresh. The Waiting card already scrolls into view (`scrollToWaiting`), so the banner is noise there. Keep it on other tabs.
- **P3-5. `.badge` permission.** `requestAuthorization` asks for `.badge`, but no payload sets a badge. Ask only for `[.alert, .sound]`, the same as wind-down.
- **P3-6. Accepted notice.** Once notifications are allowed, 05a's detail "You'll see requests here." can become `We'll let you know when Dana asks.`
- **P3-7. Lock-screen preview.** The banners show "‹Name› / Asks you to approve ending their lock early" on the lock screen. That is acceptable and contains no app or usage data. If the owner wants the stricter accountability default, set a `hiddenPreviewsBodyPlaceholder` (e.g. `Request from a trusted person`) on a category.

## Good, keep
- Push is a refresh hint only. The inbox stays authoritative, and tapping a banner opens People, where the Waiting card scrolls into view.
- Wind-down notifications behave exactly as before; only known `kind`s are handled.
- No prompt on first launch; prompts come only after Accept or Ask now, when the reason is obvious.
- The denied row only shows when someone is paired, uses no jargon, has a clear VoiceOver label, and disappears on return from Settings (`pushForegroundTick` on `.active`).
- Banners use real names and contain no IDs or app data.
- The joined banner `Joined as your trusted person.` is clear and calm.
