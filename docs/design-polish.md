# Design polish decisions

Status: collaborative visual exploration; no gallery direction is approved for production styling yet.

## Round 1 — owner feedback

- **Midnight Sanctuary** is the preferred direction.
- **Circadian Calm** is a very close second because its slightly stronger contrast improves separation and scanning.
- Circadian Calm is just barely too bright and weakens the intended bedtime experience.
- Initial production preference is **always dark**. Adaptive/light behavior may be considered later but is not approved for production yet.

## Round 2 contract

Keep the approved layout and behavior fixed. Compare:

1. **Midnight Original** — unchanged round-1 control.
2. **Midnight Contrast** — preserve the deep nighttime background while slightly increasing card/surface and boundary separation; do not approach Circadian's overall brightness.
3. **Adaptive Preview** — a future-looking Midnight-derived adaptive option that can be inspected but must not be applied to production without a separate owner decision.

Render the same Home, Get some rest and Focus time fixtures. Production changes wait for owner feedback on round two.
